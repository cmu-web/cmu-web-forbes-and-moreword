Forbes & Moreword 0.1.0 is the first release. It gives a WordPress site CMU's header, navigation and footer, from the template's own files, and the Web Kilt's components as blocks you can insert.

What's in It

  • Page, full-width, post, news and missing-page templates.
  • Six patterns from the Web Kilt, from an FAQ to numbered steps.
  • Button, ghost button and call to action styles, and headings with a line.
  • A red, gray or white roof.

Not Yet

Breadcrumbs and WordPress menus come later. For now, the navigation is your site's pages, in the order you give them.
