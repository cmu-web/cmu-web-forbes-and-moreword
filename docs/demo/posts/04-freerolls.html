Practice rolls for the 2027 Sweepstakes start Saturday, Nov. 21, at 5 a.m. on the course in Schenley Park. They run every weekend morning the roads are dry, through the end of the semester.

Before Your First Roll

  • Pass the driver safety check with your team's safety chair.
  • Sign the season waiver in the Buggy app.
  • Bring a helmet that fits. Teams have spares, but not many.

Rolls stop when the sky starts to get light, so be at the starting line by 4:45 a.m.
