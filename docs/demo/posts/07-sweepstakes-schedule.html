Preliminary heats for the 2027 Sweepstakes are Friday, April 16, and the finals are Saturday, April 17, during Spring Carnival. Both days start at 8 a.m.

How a Team Gets to the Finals

  1. Register each buggy and driver in the Buggy app.
  2. Pass the safety inspection the week before Carnival.
  3. Finish a preliminary heat with one of the 10 best times.

Teams register their buggies and drivers by Friday, Feb. 26. Heat order comes out the week before Carnival.
