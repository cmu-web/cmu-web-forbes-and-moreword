Six teams brought new buggies to Design Night on Tuesday, the most in a decade. Two have carbon fiber shells, and one weighs less than its driver's backpack.

Each team walked through its steering, brakes and wheels, then took questions from mechanics on other teams. The rest of the year, those are trade secrets.
