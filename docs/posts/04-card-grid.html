The Cards on Gray pattern is now called Card Grid, in the block inserter and in the docs. The pattern itself is the same: three cards in a gray section, each with a heading, a sentence and a call to action.

The old name described the background, and a grid of cards works on any background. Pages that already use the pattern don't change.

To insert it, open the block inserter, choose Patterns, then Carnegie Mellon Web Kilt, then Card Grid.
