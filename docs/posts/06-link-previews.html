When someone shares a link to your site in Slack, Teams or a social post, the preview shows a picture. The theme lets you choose one picture for the whole site, in Appearance › Customize › Link Previews.

For a picture of each page instead, add the FencePost Link Previews plugin. It makes a page's preview when you publish it, and the site's picture becomes the fallback. A post with a featured image uses its own.

If your site runs an SEO plugin, such as Yoast SEO, that plugin writes the previews instead, so set the picture there.
